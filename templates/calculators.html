Ostler-Smith Calculator

Variable Income Support Analysis (Bonus, Commission, Overtime)

About Ostler-Smith

In re Marriage of Ostler & Smith (1990) established that support orders can include a percentage of future variable income (bonuses, commissions, overtime) in addition to base support calculated on regular income.

This calculator helps determine the additional support owed when variable income is received.
